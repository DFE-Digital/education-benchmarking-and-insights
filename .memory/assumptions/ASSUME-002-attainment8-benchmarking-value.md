## Assumption

Providing school-level, Local Authority-level, and National Average Attainment 8 metrics alongside financial expenditure data increases user value. It enables schools, trusts, and Local Authorities to evaluate resource allocation against academic attainment outcomes in the 2026 reporting cycle.

## Context

The Financial Benchmarking and Insights Tool (FBIT) mission includes supporting better financial planning for improved pupil outcomes. Previously, only Progress 8 metrics were persisted from Key Stage 4 data. Adding Attainment 8 provides a direct measure of academic achievement across eight qualifications.

## Resolution

Pending validation through downstream reporting and user adoption in the 2026 reporting cycle.
