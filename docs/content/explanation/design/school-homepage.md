This page explains what the school homepage is for, and how far it currently meets the [service vision](/explanation/design/service-vision/). For an example, see the [school homepage for Malmesbury Primary School](https://financial-benchmarking-and-insights-tool.education.gov.uk/school/134160) on the live service.

> [!NOTE]
> **TL;DR:**
>
>* The school homepage gives a quick snapshot of a school's finances, shows where its spending differs most from similar schools, and links to the rest of the service.
>* It meets the [core purpose](/explanation/design/service-vision/#core-purpose) well, but its headline figures and RAG explanations make it less simple and quick to read than it could be.

## Who it is for

Anyone at a school involved in spending decisions, such as school business managers, headteachers, finance staff and governors. Governors are often more interested in scrutiny and holding the school to account.

Most users know more about their school's finances than the service can tell them from published data. The page is for comparing with similar schools, not for telling users about their own finances.

## Aims

1. **Give a quick snapshot of the school's finances**, such as in-year balance and revenue reserve, to put the rest of the page in context. This is not an early warning of financial difficulty.
2. **Show the main areas where the school's spending differs most from similar schools**, with as little as possible so it can be read quickly. It does not need to show what is going well.
3. **Link to the rest of the service**, most importantly benchmark spending.

## How it meets the service vision

| Part of the vision | Meeting it | Not yet meeting it |
| ------------------ | ---------- | ------------------ |
| [Core purpose](/explanation/design/service-vision/#core-purpose) | The spending section shows the cost categories where the school differs most from similar schools, with a RAG rating and how it compares, such as "higher than 93% of similar schools" | |
| [Simple, clear and easy to use](/explanation/design/service-vision/#strategic-pillars) | The cost categories where the school differs most are prominent | The headline figures show more than a snapshot needs and are visually noisy. Three paragraphs explaining RAG ratings. The number of cost categories shown, 3, was not a deliberate choice |
| [Grow national connections](/explanation/design/service-vision/#strategic-pillars) | Links to benchmark spending and the spending and costs page, where users compare with and find similar schools | The homepage does not show similar schools itself. This is deliberate for now: other pages have more room to explain why a school is worth learning from |
| [Facilitate proactivity](/explanation/design/service-vision/#strategic-pillars) | Links to find ways to spend less, and to the spending and costs page, which suggests next steps for each cost category. Next steps are deliberately left off the homepage to keep it minimal | |
