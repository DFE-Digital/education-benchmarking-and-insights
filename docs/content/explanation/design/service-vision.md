This page summarises what FBIT is for, so design and product decisions can be traced back to it. For what the service does, see the [introduction](/explanation/architecture/introduction/). For the policy objectives and users behind it, see [business context](/explanation/domain-and-methodology/business-context/).

> [!NOTE]
> **TL;DR:**
>
>* FBIT helps schools, trusts and local authorities benchmark their spending against similar organisations.
>* The aim is to turn complex data into insights and actions that support confident financial conversations and decisions.

## Vision

Help everyone who manages school, trust and local authority finances make confident decisions, by turning national data into clear insights and next steps.

## Core purpose

Help schools, trusts and local authorities benchmark against similar organisations, so they can see how they compare and learn from those organisations.

## Strategic pillars

The pillars are where we are focusing now to move towards the vision, and may change. Not every page serves every pillar.

| Pillar | What it means for the service |
| ------ | ----------------------------- |
| Grow national connections | Users compare with meaningful comparators and can find and contact organisations beyond their region to share knowledge |
| Facilitate proactivity | Users get early warning of financial risk, know what they could do next, and can easily share what they find |
| Simple, clear and easy to use | Every page is simple, clear and easy to use, so users can get what they need quickly |
